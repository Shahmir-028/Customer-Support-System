SHOPEASE REFUND POLICY

Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Digital items and gift cards are not refundable.

Cancelled orders
If an order is cancelled before shipping, the full amount is refunded automatically within 3 to 5 business days.

Refund amounts and approval
Refunds of up to 200 USD can be processed instantly by the AI support assistant. Refunds above 200 USD require approval from a human support agent, and the case is escalated with full context.

Refund timeline
After a refund is approved, the money reaches the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. The customer receives a confirmation email with a refund reference number.

Damaged or wrong items
If the item arrives damaged or is not what the customer ordered, the customer is eligible for a full refund or a free replacement. The customer should share the order number and a short description of the problem. Refund requests for damaged items are accepted up to 14 days after delivery.

Non-refundable cases
Refunds are not possible after 7 days from delivery (14 days for damaged items), for items marked as final sale, or for orders already refunded once.
