SHOPEASE SHIPPING POLICY

Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within Pakistan. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra fee. International orders take 7 to 14 business days.

Order status meanings
Processing: the order is received and being packed. This usually takes up to 24 hours.
Shipped: the order has left our warehouse and is with the courier. A tracking number is sent by email.
Delivered: the courier has handed the order to the customer.
Cancelled: the order was cancelled and will not be delivered.
Refunded: the full amount of the order was refunded to the customer.

Shipping fees
Orders above 50 USD get free standard shipping. Below 50 USD, a flat shipping fee of 3 USD applies.

Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only while the order is in Processing status. Once the order is Shipped, the address cannot be changed, and the customer should contact the courier with the tracking number.

Delayed orders
If an order has not arrived within 3 days after the estimated delivery date, the customer can ask for the case to be escalated to a human agent.
