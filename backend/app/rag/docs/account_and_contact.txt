SHOPEASE ACCOUNT AND CONTACT INFORMATION

Updating account details
Customers can ask the assistant to update their phone number or shipping address. Email address changes require verification and are handled by a human agent.

Support hours
The AI assistant is available 24/7. Human support agents are available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM Pakistan time.

Escalation to a human agent
The assistant escalates to a human agent when: the customer is angry or asks for a human; the issue involves legal threats, fraud, or payment disputes; a refund above 200 USD is requested; or the assistant cannot resolve the problem. A ticket is created with the full conversation context, and a human agent replies within 24 hours.
