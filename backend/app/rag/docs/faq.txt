SHOPEASE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I track my order?
Share your order number with the assistant and it will tell you the current status and estimated delivery time. A tracking link is also sent to your email once the order ships.

Can I cancel my order?
Yes, an order can be cancelled while it is in Processing status. Once it is Shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can refund it after delivery according to the refund policy.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit and debit cards and cash on delivery within Pakistan. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account after verification.

Do you offer a warranty?
Electronics come with a 6 month warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty does not cover physical damage or water damage. To claim warranty, contact support with your order number.

How do I get an invoice?
An invoice is emailed automatically when the order is delivered. The assistant can resend it on request.

Is my personal data safe?
We only use your data to process orders and support requests, and we never sell it to third parties.
